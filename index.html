How this flow works

  1. Write your decision question.
  2. Check how you feel.
  3. List options, then factors, then pros and cons.
  4. Pressure-test with counterarguments and bias checks.

Step 2

How do you feel about this decision?

Notice stress and emotions before you compare options.

Step 3

Add your options

List the realistic choices first. Pros and cons come later.

Step 4

Key factors

Name what matters and set how important each factor is (1–10).

Step 5

Pros, cons, and scores

For each option, capture trade-offs and score it on your factors.

Step 6

Counterarguments

Pressure-test the option that currently leads.

Write a counterargument, rate it, respond, then rate it again.
