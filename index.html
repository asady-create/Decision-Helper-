How to use it

  1. Write one clear decision question.
  2. Add two or more realistic options.
  3. List the most important pros and cons for each option.
  4. Rate every factor from 1 to 10, then review the recommendation.

2. Score key factors

Adjust factor names and importance weights to match your decision.

3. Compare options

Pros and cons help you qualify the numbers; scores help you compare consistently.
